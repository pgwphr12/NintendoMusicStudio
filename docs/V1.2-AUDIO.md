# V1.2 audio implementation and verification

GBA: third_party/viogsf/vbam/gba/{GBA.h,Sound.cpp} replaces three approximate PCM-rate kernels with sixteen nearest-rate kernels. Timer period is measured in 16,777,216 Hz CPU clocks at the FIFO DAC update. Each DAC remains an 8-bit source. The source Nyquist frequency selects the reconstruction cutoff; device rate does not change the game timer. src/audio/Resampler.hpp uses a normalized 32-tap Blackman-windowed sinc bank (256 fractional phases with interpolation) for output conversion. It retains 16 past samples, uses bounded ring lookahead, and preserves the source consumption clock across rate/speed changes. At unity 48kHz it bypasses reconstruction to retain exact PCM. DS/USF/3DS native-to-48k conversion still uses linear interpolation.

N64: LazyUSF usf_voice_tap observers capture Audio/NAudio envelope dry contributions before final summation/clipping. Envelope state RAM addresses identify stable synthesis slots. UsfVoiceTap.hpp tracks shadow DMEM through clear/load/move/mix/interleave/save; saved RAM blocks are bounded to 32 records. AI DMA resolves the actual output addresses and word-swapped stereo order. Up to 30 slots are retained; residual = original mix minus extracted dry contributions, preserving game effects and unsupported/excess contributions. Audio DMA tap and usf_render outputs are checked for sample alignment. At default unmuted gain, the exact original mix is used. Individual mute/volume applies to extracted dry signals; effects belonging to muted voices may remain in the residual effects channel. Unused slots are hidden after an activity threshold of 0.0001; once used, they remain visible for that file. Supported Audio/NAudio microcode is identified even during an initially silent passage. NEAD/MusyX/CPU software mixing is not split. Their final stereo outputs are retained. Slots are neither named MIDI instruments nor fixed N64 hardware channels.

Stream-file lanes: src/formats/StreamBackend.cpp reads and validates the BCSTM INFO track-reference table and retains its track volume/pan. When explicit BCSTM track groups exist, each group (including mono stems) becomes one independent player lane; other multichannel streams group decoded channels as stereo pairs, with an odd final channel kept mono. A two-channel file is one `STEREO MIX` lane, not separate fake left/right music tracks. Lanes are the independent PCM track groups stored by/returned from the stream decoder, not identified instruments. Summing unmuted lanes preserves the decoded mix. Stereo-only source files cannot be split into instrument stems.

The owned collection inventory has 99 BCSTM files: 67 two-channel stereo, 26 five-channel, 2 four-channel, 1 eight-channel, and 3 mono. BCSTM's track table yields 70 one-lane files, 26 three-lane files, 2 two-lane files, and 1 four-lane file. Examples: ATHLETIC 3 tracks, MENU 4 tracks, BIG_MARIO 1 mix lane. Wii/GC inventory: 88 BRSTM, 2 BNS, 9 ADX; 18 BRSTM expose multiple decoded stereo groups, while the others decode to one mix lane.

Tests: authored GBA DMA FIFO drivers at 10512/16384 Hz; real HLE envelope/interleave/save/DMA separation, reconstruction and clearing; authored five-channel BCSTM with two stereo track groups and one mono stem; downsampling alias attenuation. All 47 multi-lane stream files in the owned folder passed bounded six-second render, finite-sample, seek/replay, and all-lane mute checks. Existing output clock, rates, speed, waveform controls, reverb, native UI, fullscreen and folder/autoplay tests remain. This is bounded stream coverage, not whole-song or instrument identity proof. Commercial samples are not bundled.
